[Events](/docs/concepts/events) are the raw log records emitted by the Web, Node.js, Swift, and Android SDKs — every `Pulse.info()`, `Pulse.error()`, `Pulse.step()`, etc. Filter by app, level, user, session, environment, screen, device, OS, or time range. Defaults to the last 24 hours. The device fields are platform-shaped: `device_model` is the hardware model on a native app and the browser with its major version (`Chrome 120`) on web, `os_version` is the OS version (`18.0`) or the OS name and version (`macOS 10.15.7`), and `screen_name` is the screen name or the URL path (`/checkout/payment`).
