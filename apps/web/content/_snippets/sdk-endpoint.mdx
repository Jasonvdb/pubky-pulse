In the browser and Node SDKs, from `0.2.0` on, `endpoint` is optional. Leave it out and the SDK falls back to `https://ingest.pubkypulse.com` — [Pubky's own hosted instance](/docs/getting-started/hosted) — and that fallback is silent: nothing is logged, warned, or thrown. So if you run your own Pubky Pulse, you **must** pass your ingest host explicitly; forget it and your data goes to Pubky's instance instead of yours, with nothing to tell you.

The Swift and Android SDKs have no such fallback. `endpoint` is required there, so pass your ingest host on every `configure()` call.
